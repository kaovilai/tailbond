# "Get tailbond" page: persona task script

The home server ("homebox") serves a page on its Tailscale address,
http://homebox/ ("Get tailbond"), for devices that run Tailscale but not
tailbond yet. It has a switcher: Android · Mac · iPhone · Linux. You see it
only as the screenshots listed in [screens.md](screens.md); you can't click
anything, but you can say where you'd tap and what you'd expect.

Tasks:

1. **On your Mac, install tailbond from homebox's page.** You're on your
   MacBook with Tailscale on; someone told you "go to homebox/ in Safari".
   Get tailbond installed and the Mac connected to homebox.
2. **From your Mac, get the app onto your Android phone.** Still at the Mac,
   you want tailbond on your Android phone too.

For each task: what you'd do step by step, where you'd hesitate or get it
wrong, and anything missing, confusing or wrong. Rate each point **high**
(you'd fail or give up), **medium** (you'd get stuck or need help), or
**low** (polish). End with a one-line verdict per task.
