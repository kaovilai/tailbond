# Round 1: persona A, "GL traveller"

A consultant who travels monthly with a GL.iNet Slate 7 and an Android phone. Knows GL's admin panel (Repeater, VPN Dashboard, firmware upgrades) but isn't a network engineer. A relative set up the Fedora home server. Wants hotels to just work, calls not to drop, work traffic not to leak, and no surprises from updates.

Screens used:
- GL panel: r01, r02 (desktop and phone), r03, r04, r05, r06, r07, r10, s01
- Web dashboard: w01, w02, w03, w05, w06, w07, w09, w10, w11, w12
- Android: 02, 10, 11, 30, 37

## Task 1: Make sure everything stays up to date

**Done?** Partly. I can read each device's version (Slate 7 in its Updates card, homebox in its dashboard, phone under Settings → Updates). I can update the Slate 7 and the phone myself. I can't update homebox from where I am, and I can't tell whether it updates by itself. Screens: r01, r02, r02-phone, r03, r04, r05, r07, s01, w01, w02, w03, w09, android 02, 10, 11.

**Findings**

1. **high**, `w01-homebox-dnf-update-available-desktop.png`: The only way to update homebox is to run `sudo dnf upgrade tailbond-server` in a terminal on the Fedora PC. I'm in a hotel, I don't use Linux, and my relative set the PC up, so I can't do this. The page has no button and no next step for someone who can't use a terminal. **Suggestion:** offer an "Update now" that runs the dnf upgrade through the system (for example polkit), or at least say "Ask whoever manages this PC to run this, or turn on dnf-automatic once", with a link that explains how.
2. **medium**, `w01-homebox-dnf-update-available-desktop.png` / `w02-updated-devices-out-of-date-backups-desktop.png`: "Turn on dnf-automatic to get them by themselves" doesn't say whether dnf-automatic is already on. The policy offers only Tell me / Off, so I'd assume homebox never updates by itself. **Suggestion:** show the actual state: "Automatic updates: on (dnf-automatic, next run 06:00)" or "off".
3. **medium**, `r02-router-update-available-desktop.png` (and `r02-router-update-available-phone.png`): "Update available" is only in the Updates card at the very bottom of a long page, below Tailnet machines. The top "Connected to homebox" card and the GL sidebar show nothing. On the phone layout it takes many screens of scrolling to reach. **Suggestion:** add a line or badge to the top status card ("Update available: 0.2.0"), and a dot on the VPN → tailbond menu entry.
4. **medium**, `r05-router-automatic-desktop.png`: Automatic "installs a new version by itself… devices drop for a few seconds", but it never says when. If it restarts during a client video call, that is exactly the surprise I want to avoid. **Suggestion:** let me pick a quiet hour (default about 03:00 local time), show it ("installs at 03:00"), and skip installing while traffic is heavy.
5. **medium**, `r04-router-rolled-back-desktop.png`: After "0.2.0 didn't start here… the tunnel didn't come back within 60 seconds", the card offers "Update now" again with no advice. At a hotel the cause could be the Wi-Fi, not the new version. I can't tell whether retrying is safe, or whether Automatic would keep retrying and keep dropping my calls. **Suggestion:** add "Try again on a stable connection, or wait for the next version". Also say what Automatic will do: "Automatic won't try 0.2.0 again".
6. **medium**, `w09-vps-check-failed-desktop.png`: The chip and headline say "Up to date" in green, but the last check failed ("Couldn't reach GitHub: api.github.com: i/o timeout"). I'd read green and stop looking. **Suggestion:** use a neutral or amber chip, "Couldn't check", plus "last successful check: <time>", and keep the raw error in smaller text.
7. **medium**, `s01-flint-devices-out-of-date-desktop.png` / `w01-homebox-dnf-update-available-desktop.png`: "2 devices out of date" lists slate7 and pixel-8. beryl, which is off, isn't mentioned, so I can't tell whether it's current. **Suggestion:** list it as "beryl, travel router: off, last seen on 0.1.0 (Oct 31)".
8. **medium**, `w01-homebox-dnf-update-available-desktop.png` / `w03-vps-update-available-desktop.png`: Nothing says whether devices on 0.1.0 still work with a 0.2.x server, so I can't judge how urgent "out of date" is. w03's "All 3 devices are on 0.1.0 or newer" tells me nothing actionable. **Suggestion:** say "Devices on 0.1.0 keep working with this server", or "slate7 must update by <version> or it stops connecting".
9. **medium**, `02-settings-update-available-row.png` / `11-updates-available.png`: On the phone, the update shows only in Settings, at the bottom of a long page. None of the screens shows it on the main screen or as a notification. I rarely open Settings. **Suggestion:** add a small banner on the main screen and/or one Android notification per new version.
10. **medium**, `11-updates-available.png`: "Download update" sends me to the browser, which asks me to let it install apps. Nothing says whether the tunnel drops while the app is replaced, whether it comes back on by itself, or whether work traffic goes out unprotected in between. **Suggestion:** add one line: "tailbond turns itself back on after the update; while it installs, apps are <blocked / on the normal connection>".
11. **low**, `r02-router-update-available-desktop.png` / `11-updates-available.png`: Version strings differ: "0.1.0" in the card, "tailbond 0.1.0-r42" in the footer, "v0.2.0" on the phone, "0.2.0" elsewhere. I compare versions across devices, so the mismatch makes me double-check. **Suggestion:** use one format everywhere ("0.2.0"), and drop or explain "-r42".
12. **low**, `w01-homebox-dnf-update-available-desktop.png` / `w02-updated-devices-out-of-date-backups-desktop.png`: The tab badge shows "3" on one screen and "2" on the other, and nothing says what it counts. **Suggestion:** a tooltip ("1 update for this server, 2 devices out of date"), or a dot instead of a number.

## Task 2: Move the home server to a new machine using a backup

**Done?** Partly. I can make a backup (w05) and the restore preview is clear about what changes (w06). But the end screen tells me I'm finished when I'm not. No screen covers my real case, a Fedora PC at home moving to another PC. The device side is unclear, especially with the travel router's kill switch on. Screens: w02, w05, w06, w07, w10, w11, w12, s01, r10, android 02, 30, 37.

**Findings**

13. **high**, `w07-restore-done-desktop.png`: "Restored… Devices reconnect by themselves too." contradicts the restore preview (w06). The preview says I must first set the new public address in the Devices tab, and devices paired with a link need a new link. I'd click Done and leave, and my router and phone would stay disconnected. **Suggestion:** replace the line with a checklist of the remaining steps ("1. Set the public address, 2. Send new links to: pixel-8"), with live per-device status ("slate7: reconnected / waiting").
14. **high**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png`: "Devices then pick it up by themselves within a few hours." My Slate 7 has the kill switch on (r01), so for those hours every device behind it at the hotel has no internet, and nothing warns me. **Suggestion:** state the effect ("travel routers with the kill switch on block traffic until they reconnect"). Give a way to move devices now (for example "Ask to connect again" on the router), or tell me to keep the old server running until each device shows it has moved.
15. **medium**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png`: The router restore (r10) warns that "both can't use it at once… turn tailbond off there first". The server restore has no such warning, though the old server would still have the same keys and devices. I'd leave the old PC running. **Suggestion:** add the same warning, with when to turn the old one off.
16. **medium**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png`: "Devices that were paired with a link and have no Tailscale… send them a new link" assumes I know how each device was paired. I don't know whether my pixel-8 was added by QR. **Suggestion:** name the devices: "pixel-8: needs a new link. slate7, beryl: find the new server by themselves."
17. **medium**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png`: Devices are said to find the new server "because vps has a Tailscale name (vps.tail1234.ts.net)". Nothing says the new machine must be signed into the same Tailscale account under that name, or that the old one must be removed first. Otherwise the new one may get a different name. The router dialog (r10) does say Tailscale isn't in the backup; this one doesn't. **Suggestion:** add "Tailscale isn't in the backup: sign this machine in to <account>, and remove the old 'vps' first so it keeps the same name".
18. **medium**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png` / `w10-vps-new-machine-desktop.png`: Every restore screen is VPS-to-VPS. My move is a home PC to another home PC, where the likely extra step is pointing the home internet router's port forward (UDP 443) at the new PC. Nothing mentions it, though s01 (Flint) explains port forwarding elsewhere. **Suggestion:** for a home server, have the preview say "If your internet router forwards UDP 443 to the old PC, change it to this one", and confirm with a setup-health check after the restore.
19. **medium**, `w10-vps-new-machine-desktop.png`: The "new machine before restoring" looks like a running old server: Connected, 5 d uptime, 7.5 GB data, "All 3 devices are on 0.2.0 or newer", and six saved backups including slate7 and beryl. I can't tell which machine I'm on, so I could restore onto the wrong one. **Suggestion:** a fresh machine should say so plainly ("New server: no devices yet. Restore a backup to move an existing setup here"), and both machines should show their name or address prominently.
20. **medium**, `w02-updated-devices-out-of-date-backups-desktop.png` / `w12-whats-inside-desktop.png`: The old homebox holds the travel routers' saved backups (slate7, beryl). "What's in a backup" doesn't list them, so they're probably lost when I move, and they're my safety net if the Slate 7 is reset on a trip. **Suggestion:** include them in the server backup, or warn before a move ("Download slate7's and beryl's backups first").
21. **medium**, `w05-backup-download-dialog-desktop.png` / `w11-scheduled-backups-change-desktop.png` / `w02-updated-devices-out-of-date-backups-desktop.png`: There are several passphrases: one per manual download, one for automatic backups, and each device's own ("Each is locked with the passphrase it was made with"). Changing the automatic-backups passphrase (w11) doesn't say that older backups keep the old one. By restore day I won't know which one opens which file. **Suggestion:** show which passphrase a file uses ("automatic-backup passphrase, set Oct 12"). Add an optional hint field, and a "Test passphrase" button on saved backups.
22. **medium**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png` / `r10-router-restore-preview-desktop.png`: File names and dates disagree: "vps-2026-11-01T0300.tbbackup" says "made Nov 2, 16:00", and "slate7-2026-11-01.tbbackup" says "made Nov 2, 17:00". When I pick among daily backups by file name, this risks restoring the wrong day. **Suggestion:** make the file name use the same date and time as "made".
23. **low**, `w06-restore-preview-new-vps-desktop.png` / `w12-whats-inside-desktop.png`: Raw values show up where the rest of the UI uses plain words: "Tunnel addresses 10.77.0.0/24 → 10.83.142.0/24", "Approvers sam@example.com" (who is sam?), "Mode speed", "Updates notify", "Router settings (UCI) no". **Suggestion:** use the UI's own labels ("Fastest", "Tell me"), explain "Approvers" in a few words, and hide address ranges behind "Details".
24. **low**, `37-restore-a-server-backup.png`: "Restore it there" doesn't say where. **Suggestion:** "Open homebox's dashboard → Updates & backup → Restore from a backup."
25. **low**, `w11-scheduled-backups-change-desktop.png`: The page header ("tailbond · Home server · reachable at…") is drawn over the middle of the Updates card, covering the "Routers: Update now…" line and the "When a new version is out" heading. This may only be a capture artifact, but as rendered it hides text. **Suggestion:** check the sticky header against scrolled content and keep it at the top.
26. **low**, `w02-updated-devices-out-of-date-backups-desktop.png`: "Keep a copy somewhere other than this machine too" gives no way to do it except downloading each file. If the old PC dies, its automatic backups die with it. **Suggestion:** offer a target for automatic copies (USB drive, cloud folder, or the Flint/travel router), as the routers already do with "a copy also goes to homebox".

## Task 3: The router got a GL firmware update. Is tailbond still there?

**Done?** Yes, if I scroll to the bottom: r06 says "The GL firmware update removed tailbond; it reinstalled itself with your settings… Nothing to do". It doesn't answer the question I care about most. Screens: r06, r01, r05.

**Findings**

27. **medium**, `r06-router-reinstalled-after-firmware-upgrade-desktop.png`: The reinstall notice is only in the Updates card at the very bottom. The top status card ("Connected to homebox") and GL's own VPN Dashboard say nothing. After a firmware upgrade I'd glance at the top, see "Connected", and never learn that tailbond was gone for a while. **Suggestion:** show a dismissible line in the top card for a day ("Reinstalled after the GL firmware update at 21:36").
28. **medium**, `r06-router-reinstalled-after-firmware-upgrade-desktop.png`: Nothing says what happened to my devices between the firmware flash and the reinstall. tailbond, and so its kill switch, was removed. Did my laptop's work traffic go out over the hotel Wi-Fi unprotected during that gap? **Suggestion:** state it: "Between 21:31 and 21:36 tailbond wasn't installed; devices here used <the normal internet / no internet>". If traffic did go out unprotected, say so and suggest turning off Wi-Fi clients before a firmware upgrade.
29. **medium**, `r06-router-reinstalled-after-firmware-upgrade-desktop.png` / `r01-router-up-to-date-desktop.png`: No screen says what the self-reinstall depends on, or what I'd see if it failed. For example: must GL's "Keep settings" be ticked during the upgrade? Does it need internet, which a hotel sign-in page could block? I only learn about this feature after an upgrade has gone well. **Suggestion:** add a line in the Updates card before any upgrade happens ("GL firmware upgrades: keep 'Keep settings' ticked; tailbond reinstalls itself once online"). Also give a failure state that points to "Restore from a backup" or the saved copy on homebox.
30. **low**, `r06-router-reinstalled-after-firmware-upgrade-desktop.png`: "Now on 0.2.0, since Nov 3, 21:36" uses a date where the rest of the page uses relative times. "Checked 2 h ago" is older than the reinstall, so I can't tell whether the reinstalled version has been checked against the latest release. **Suggestion:** "since today 21:36 (24 min ago)", and run a check right after a reinstall.
