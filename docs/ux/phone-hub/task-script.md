# Phone hub: persona task script

tailbond bonds several internet connections into one tunnel to your home
server ("homebox"). The Android app does this on the phone (mobile data +
Wi-Fi + nearby phones). **Phone hub** lets a Mac use the phone's bonded
connection through a proxy. You see the product only as the screenshots in
[screens.md](screens.md) and the text excerpts listed there; you can't
click, but say where you'd tap and what you'd expect.

Setting: your Mac and your Android phone (Pixel 9) are both paired with
homebox, and tailbond is installed on both. You also have an iPhone.

Tasks:

1. **At a hotel, get your Mac onto your Android phone's bonded connection.**
   The hotel Wi-Fi is slow. Tailbond on the Pixel already bonds its 5G with
   the hotel Wi-Fi. Get the Mac's browsing to go through the Pixel's bonded
   connection, and know how to undo it.
2. **Use your iPhone as the hub over USB.** Another day, only your iPhone
   is with you. Use it for the Mac, over the USB cable.

For each task: what you'd do step by step, where you'd hesitate or get it
wrong, and anything missing, confusing or wrong. Rate each point **high**
(you'd fail or give up), **medium** (you'd get stuck or need help), or
**low** (polish). End with a one-line verdict per task.
