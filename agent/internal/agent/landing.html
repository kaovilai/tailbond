3

Turn tailbond on

Once approved, tailbond shows what it will do: tap Add phone. Then tap the big switch at the top of tailbond. tailbond explains that it takes over from Tailscale, and Android asks once to allow its connection: tap OK.

Android runs one VPN at a time, so while tailbond is on, Tailscale's VPN is off on this phone. Your Tailscale machines stay reachable through {{.Server}}.

tailbond connects straight to {{.Server}} over the internet (not through Tailscale), and by default this phone's internet traffic goes out from {{.Server}}, like using it as an exit node. tailbond's Settings change that. To go back, tap Switch back to Tailscale in tailbond.
